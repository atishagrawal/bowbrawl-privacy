Bow Brawl: Archery Duel — Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

Bow Brawl: Archery Duel ("the game") is made by Atish Agrawal, an independent developer. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: the game does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal data.

What the game collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no online features. It does not connect to the internet and never sends any information about you or your device to the developer or to anyone else.

Data kept on your device

Your game progress (levels completed, stars, coins, upgrades and settings) is saved only on your own device so you can carry on where you left off. The developer cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes it permanently.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device, for example when your energy has refilled. They are created and shown entirely on the device; no notification data is sent anywhere. You can turn them off at any time in your device's settings.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. When you download the game, Google Play handles the download under Google's Privacy Policy, which the developer does not control.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds a feature that uses personal data (for example ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.
